Step-by-Step

Run it on
any stock.

Follow these steps in order. Each workflow takes 5–7 minutes.

Phase 01 — Earnings Reviewer
1
Get the earnings call transcript
Go to screener.in → search the company → scroll to Concalls section → download the latest transcript PDF. For US stocks, use the company IR page or seekingalpha.com. You want the Q-on-Q transcript, not just the press release.
2
Open your AI tool and paste this prompt
Copy the prompt below. Paste it into Claude or ChatGPT. Then paste the transcript below it (or upload the PDF if supported).
Earnings Reviewer Prompt
You are a senior equity analyst reviewing an earnings call transcript. My investment thesis is: [write your thesis in 2–3 sentences here] Analyse the transcript below and give me: 1. WHAT MANAGEMENT COMMITTED TO — Specific guidance, targets, timelines they mentioned 2. WHAT THEY AVOIDED OR DEFLECTED — Questions they were vague on or redirected 3. LANGUAGE SHIFTS — Compare tone to what you'd expect. Any signs of increased caution? 4. THESIS CHECK — Based only on this call: does my thesis still hold? Yes / No / Partially — What's the single biggest risk to my thesis from this call? Be direct. Skip filler. Flag anything that surprised you. [PASTE TRANSCRIPT BELOW THIS LINE]
💡 Write your thesis before you paste the transcript. If you can't write it in 2–3 sentences, you don't have a thesis yet — you have a hope.
3
Act on the output
Don't just read it. Open a note and write: "Thesis status after Q[X]: [holds / shaken / broken]. Why: [one line]." Date it. This becomes your decision log — the thing most retail investors never build.
Phase 02 — Market Researcher
4
Enable web search in your AI tool
Claude.ai has web search built in (look for the globe icon). ChatGPT Plus has it too. On free tiers, you can paste recent news headlines manually — or use Perplexity.ai which searches live by default and is free.
5
Run the portfolio brief prompt
Use this for each stock you actively hold (not your entire 100-stock portfolio — the ones you track closely).
Market Researcher Prompt
Stock: [Company Name] ([NSE/BSE ticker]) I hold this position. Give me an investor brief for the last 30 days. Cover: 1. KEY NEWS — 3–5 most relevant developments (ignore routine noise) 2. ANALYST UPDATES — Any rating changes, target price revisions, notable reports 3. UPCOMING CATALYSTS — Results date, AGM, sector events, macro triggers 4. RISK FLAGS — Anything that could move this stock down 10%+ in the next quarter 5. ONE-LINE VERDICT — Should I be paying closer attention right now? Why? Use web search. Be concise. I want signal, not summaries.
6
Run this weekly for your top 5–8 holdings
Pick a day — Monday morning works well. Run through your active positions. Takes 15–20 minutes total. You'll catch more in this 20 minutes than most people catch in a week of scrolling financial news.
Phase 03 — Model Builder
7
Export financials from Screener.in
Go to screener.in → search company → scroll to the financial tables. You want:
  • Profit & Loss (last 5 years)
  • Balance Sheet (last 3 years)
  • Cash Flow statement (last 3 years)
  • Key Ratios (ROE, ROCE, P/E, Debt/Equity)
Copy the data as plain text and paste it into your AI prompt. Or click "Export to Excel" and pull the numbers.
8
Run the valuation prompt
Model Builder Prompt
Company: [Name] | Sector: [Sector] | Current Price: ₹[X] I want a structured valuation analysis. Here is the financial data: [PASTE SCREENER DATA HERE] Build me: 1. FAIR VALUE RANGE — Bear case / Base case / Bull case (with key assumption for each) — Use P/E or EV/EBITDA — whichever is more appropriate for this sector 2. GROWTH ASSUMPTIONS — What revenue/profit growth are each scenario assuming? — Are these assumptions realistic vs historical performance? 3. RISK BREAKDOWN — Top 3 risks, ranked by probability × impact — Which risk could permanently impair the business? 4. KEY VARIABLE — What's the single number I should track most closely going forward? 5. VERDICT — At current price, which scenario am I essentially betting on? Show your working. Be honest about what's uncertain.
⚠️ This is not a buy signal. It's a thinking framework. The model is only as good as the inputs — and the future is never in the data. Use this to stress-test your assumptions, not to outsource your judgment.
9
Document your scenario and revisit quarterly
Write down which scenario (bear/base/bull) you're betting on at the time you buy. Revisit after each earnings. If the business has moved away from your base case, your position size should respond to that — not your emotions.
Going Deeper

What makes
this actually work.

The tools are free. The edge isn't the tool — it's how you use it.

01
Thesis before analysis
Write your thesis before you run any workflow. If you don't know why you own something, AI will just generate elegant-sounding confusion. The thesis is your anchor — everything else is a check against it.
02
Verify what matters
AI can hallucinate numbers. Cross-check key figures (revenue, EPS, dates) against the actual BSE filing or Screener.in. Use AI for pattern recognition and framing — not as the source of record.
03
Build a decision log
The real advantage isn't the research — it's the record. Date every analysis. Write what you believed and why. When you're wrong, you'll know why. That feedback loop is what turns information into learning.
04
AI speeds up thinking, not replaces it
The best analysts who use these tools still do the thinking. AI processes information faster than you can read. The quality of your question determines the quality of the output. Better prompts = better research.
