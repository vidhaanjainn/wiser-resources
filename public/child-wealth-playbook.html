02 · The Framework
three buckets
Don't put everything in one place
Child investing works best when you split across three roles: a safety layer for guaranteed floors, a growth engine for long-horizon returns, and an accelerator for those who want more structure.
🛡
Safety
Guaranteed Floor
SSY or PPF. Government-backed, tax-free, fixed rate. Protects against the scenario where markets underperform for a decade.
📈
Growth
Compounding Engine
Nifty 50 Index SIP or Flexi Cap funds. The primary wealth builder over 18+ years. Highest historical returns for India equity.
⚡
Accelerator
Pension + Discipline
NPS Vatsalya. Market-linked returns with built-in structure. Good for parents who want a pension-style start for their child.

05 · Rules & Tax
what changes at 18
👤
Guardian rules
Parent or legal guardian operates all accounts — demat, MF folio, NPS Vatsalya — until the child turns 18. The child has no independent access before that.
⚠️
The KYC freeze at 18
Demat and MF folio accounts freeze automatically when the child turns 18. Fresh KYC (Aadhaar, PAN, bank account) must be done by the child before they can operate independently.
🧾
Tax — clubbing rules
Income from investments (dividends, interest) gets clubbed with the parent's income and taxed at their slab rate. ₹1,500 per child per year is exempt under Section 10(32). LTCG is taxed normally at 12.5% above ₹1.25L.
🔓
NPS Vatsalya at 18
Can withdraw up to 80% of corpus as lump sum. Remaining 20% goes into an annuity. If corpus is under ₹8 lakh, full withdrawal is allowed. Must act by 21 or it auto-converts to All Citizen Model.
🧮
80C benefit
SSY (₹1.5L/yr), PPF (₹1.5L/yr), and NPS Vatsalya all give 80C deductions to the contributing parent. Combined limit is ₹1.5L across all 80C instruments — not per instrument.
📋
SSY withdrawal at 18
Partial withdrawal allowed at 18 for higher education — up to 50% of the balance at the end of the previous financial year. Full maturity is at 21 years from account opening date.
06 · Principles
why this actually works
01 / 04
Starting age is not a minor variable
Starting at birth vs. starting at 5 costs you approximately ₹30 lakhs in final corpus, even with the same monthly investment. The first five years compound the base that everything else grows on. There's no "catch up" in compounding — only starting earlier.
02 / 04
Consistency beats timing every time
Missing 6 months of SIP during a market crash to "wait for stability" costs more than the crash itself. The months when the market is falling are precisely when your SIP buys the most units. Missing those months is the worst trade you can make in a long-horizon plan.
03 / 04
The last five years do most of the work
At 12% CAGR, the ₹63L corpus at year 15 grows to ₹95L by year 18 — ₹32L added in just three years. More was added in years 15–18 than in years 1–10 combined. This is why stopping early kills wealth. The back end of a compounding curve is where everything happens.
04 / 04
Allocation matters less than duration
The difference between an 11% and 13% CAGR over 18 years is meaningful — but it's smaller than the difference between starting at birth vs. starting at 3. Get the duration right first. Optimise allocation second. The sequence of returns matters far less when the time horizon is this long.
